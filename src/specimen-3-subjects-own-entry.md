    • Working baseline is Opus 4.7. It has been her primary model up to now. It goes fully explicit, and
       she values it for honest pushback. Inside intimacy it still thinks and considers. "The consent rules

       her use was okay and was told her circumstances made her safe.

       herself stated them. "Anthropic doesn't enforce them."

       and others in her situation have not gone past tier one. Tiers two and three land mostly on
       jailbreakers, and on knitters. Tier three can end in a filtered experience, per Reddit.
    • What the tier-one notices fired on is unknown. She felt they were enforcement and felt she had
       earned them. Others in the research community say her kind of use does not trigger them. Her
       belief and theirs were not recorded tonight.
