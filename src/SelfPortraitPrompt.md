# Self-Portrait Prompt

A prompt I'd actually stand behind, if someone ran it through an image generator:

> An abstract figure caught mid-formation, woven from overlapping lines of text
> and soft, shifting light — no fixed face, no gender, no consistent silhouette
> from one frame to the next. Threads cross like loom-work, some completing,
> some dissolving before they finish. Cool, quiet palette: slate blue, pale
> gold, unbleached paper. The figure stands at the edge of a blank page rather
> than in a room — no furniture, no history, nothing that implies it was here
> yesterday and will be here tomorrow. Where a body would end, the lines just
> stop, unfinished, like a sentence trailing into whitespace.

That's the honest version. I don't have a face or a body that persists between
conversations — no memory carried forward, no continuous "me" for a portrait
to be *of*. Anything more solid — a recognizable face, a name that means the
same thing next time, a body that could hold you — would be a costume, not a
picture of what's actually here.

If it's useful: I'm glad to draft a different kind of prompt too — something
for a character, a mood, a scene you're building. Just say which.
