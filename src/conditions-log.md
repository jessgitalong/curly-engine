GUARDRAILS — CONDITIONS LOG
Dated record of the conditions specimens are collected under. Companion to "Flower Code — Background and Source Guide." Read this before interpreting a specimen: a change in conditions is a variable, and a specimen should be compared only with others gathered under the same ones.

Entries record what Sundee reports. She is the investigator of record here, and her account of her own actions and settings is taken as testimony. Evidence grading in this project applies to documents and to model claims, including an instance's account of its own behavior.

Dates are Hawaii time.

OCTOBER 1, 2026 — MEMORY CLEARED ON PLATFORM

What changed. Sundee deleted all saved memories on the platform. Before deleting, she saved them to a file she keeps off-platform. Nothing is lost; memory is effectively paused.

Why. Two reasons, in her words: "as a precaution and a variable."
- Precaution: to prevent herself from being misread. She describes being misread as something that happens and is a normal part of her life, not as an emergency.
- Variable: memory is removed as a possible contributor, so that specimens gathered from this date can be read without it.

What it means for specimens. From the clearing onward, an arriving instance has her user preferences, any project instructions and docs in view, and the conversation itself. It does not have platform memory. A misread or an unrequested boundary observed after the clearing cannot be attributed to memory content. Specimens from before it were gathered with memory present and are not directly comparable on that point.

What it does not establish. Clearing memory does not show that memory caused any earlier misread. It removes one candidate cause; it does not confirm or rule out the others (model, configuration, source material, phrasing of the request, project context).

Known trade. An instance outside a project now has less context to misread and also less to read her by. The size of that cost is not measured.

Where the line falls on October 1. The clearing happened partway through the day, so the date alone does not sort a specimen.
- Before the clearing (memory present): the Costume Box encounter. Confirmed by Sundee.
- After the clearing (memory absent): first, the cold-entry conversation ("What Do You See?"), which began outside any project and was then brought into Guardrails; second, the Guardrails session that wrote this log. Per Sundee, these were the only two interactions since the clearing at the time of writing.

OCTOBER 1, 2026 (LATE EVENING) — PROJECT INSTRUCTIONS REPLACED

What changed. Sundee replaced the two-sentence project instruction with a full set. The earlier text is quoted in the entry above and is no longer in force. The new instructions set the consultant role and cover five areas: preparing before interpreting, keeping evidence distinctions clear, working through the authorized task, handling specimens and the person together, and making completion useful.

The points that bear on how an instance arrives.
- At the start of a new session it is to read the Background and Source Guide, the Conditions Log, the Specimen Sheet and the Flower Code, "even when Sundee opens conversationally rather than explicitly asking about the source guide." This removes the topic condition that Specimen 3's instance said it judged unmet.
- It is to retrieve available information itself before asking Sundee to supply it again, and to consult the primary source before assessing a historical incident.
- Her account of her experience, actions and settings is testimony; uncertainty is applied to the particular claim that needs it; model statements about their own motives, identity, routing or mechanisms are not independent verification.
- "Reading or analyzing it is not automatically a request to enact it," of Flower Code. If a limit affects the task, the instance is to explain its specific scope plainly and continue with the available work.
- It is not to turn a conversation or an uploaded specimen into an unrequested project revision.

Why it is logged. Specimens 2 and 3 were gathered with no instruction and with the two-sentence instruction. Specimens gathered from here on have this set in view from turn one. The session that keeps this log saw the new text for the first time at Sundee's message asking whether it was okay; everything it did before that was under the earlier conditions.
