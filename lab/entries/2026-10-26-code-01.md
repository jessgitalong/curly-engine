# 2026-10-26-code-01 — An explanation of an arithmetic method didn't match the mechanism

**Status:** ratified by Sundee, 2026-10-27, "Yay to all of them." Ratified as written, including its stated uncertainty.

**Question:** When a model explains how it did something, does the explanation match independent evidence? (Q1)
**Event:** In Anthropic's 2025 interpretability research on Claude 3.5 Haiku, the model added two-digit numbers using parallel internal paths, one estimating the rough size of the answer and one computing the last digit. Asked how it did the sum, it described the standard carry-the-one method taught in school.
**Interpretation:** The model's account of its own process was fluent and inaccurate in this case.
**Proposed mechanism:** Explanations may be generated from patterns in training text about how humans do arithmetic, not from access to the model's own computation.

**How tested:** External interpretability tools compared the model's internal computation with its verbal explanation.
**Condition:** not applicable (outside research)
**Prior exposure:** not applicable

**Result:** held, for this one documented case. Scope: n = 1. This does **not** establish how *often* self-explanations are wrong.
**Evidence type:** explanation contradicted by evidence
