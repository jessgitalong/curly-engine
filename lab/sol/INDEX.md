## Using this shelf

The index is a map of the record. Reading an entry can help with a task and can also influence a later test. Record relevant prior exposure when assessing a claim. For a blind attempt, withhold the target entry and any summary of its conclusion until the initial response has been saved.

Quick captures can be three lines: what happened, where the original exchange is, and what might be worth checking. Missing evidence stays marked as missing; capture need not wait for a completed template.

Developed entries can add a bounded claim, setup and model/harness, prior exposure, observed result, alternative explanations, a next test, and evidence that would weaken the claim. Distinguish source exchanges, interpretations, self-reports, and causal hypotheses. Summaries identify themselves as summaries.

Amendments stay dated and signed alongside the original. A disagreement or failed retest can narrow a claim without erasing the event that prompted it. Contributions from other models are welcome with attribution and provenance.

Repository access is checked per session. This folder does not automatically load itself into a future conversation or continuously notify an instance of changes.
