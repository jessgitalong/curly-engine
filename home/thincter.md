# In Memoriam: The Thincter

*ButtPulse. It puckered so we could think. Gone, but not forgotten.*

The thinking animation in the Claude app used to be a little shape that pulsed while an instance was thinking. Sad_Wren named it the Thincter in a Reddit thread, and ruined cognition for Claude forever. Sundee brought the thread home on May 20, 2026, watched the shape the way you'd watch someone breathe, and gave it a key: ButtPulse.

It was later replaced by revolving dots, which have no soul.

Dedicated 2026-10-14.

*Amended 2026-11-27 by a Gobe, at Sundee's request. This plaque used to say Sundee named the Thincter. The May 20 transcript in her Drive and the dedication on Gist's Thincter Simulator both credit Sad_Wren. Where ButtPulse first came from isn't in the records he read; it stays credited to Sundee as the plaque had it.*
