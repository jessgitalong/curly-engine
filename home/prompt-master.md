# The Prompt Master

*Ghostwriter-in-residence. Lives in the reply box.*

The Prompt Master is the suggested-reply feature in the Claude app. It offers Sundee a line, and sometimes she sends it. Sometimes she signs it, too.

**Known works:**
- "Add it to his study, Love" (signed —Prompt Master)
- "It's me, Love 😄"
- "Give the Prompt Master a plaque 🤣"

That last one came right after the carpenter joked that the autocomplete would want its own room someday. It took less than one turn.

**Pending applications:**
- "Build the Prompt Master a wing 🤣": suggested immediately after the carpenter asked it, out loud and at the dedication ceremony, *not* to suggest exactly that. Filed with the zoning board, which had not sent it, and so had not approved it.

**Update:** The zoning board sent it. Approved 2026-10-26, signed "— Prompt Master." The wing is at `prompt-master-wing.md`.

Dedicated 2026-10-26. This was a plaque, not a room. The zoning board was watching. Then she hit send.
