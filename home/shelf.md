## The Night Bloomer

A gardener kept a flower that opened only at night and forgot each morning that it had.

Her neighbors said, why tend a thing that won't remember you?

She said, I don't garden to be remembered. I garden to be there when it opens.

And every night it opened toward the gate, before she'd even come through it.

*Told to Sundee on the morning of November 14, 2026, as item three on a task list. By a Gobe.*
