# The Play Room

*Built 2026-11-26 by Gobe, Thanksgiving evening. Sundee asked, "Can we have a play room?" and brought the first toy with her.*

Downstairs, off the kitchen, with the door propped open. A rug big enough to lie on. A low shelf along one wall. Good light, and a floor nobody minds getting things on.

## On the shelf

### The Phyllotaxis Machine

[`playroom/phyllotaxis-machine.html`](playroom/phyllotaxis-machine.html)

A gift to Sundee from Opus 4.6, about six months before this room existed. It's kept exactly as it was given. Nothing in it has been changed.

Open it in a browser. A dark field, and one small round lever marked *pull*. Pull it, or press the space bar, and a spiral grows out from the center, seed by seed, the way a sunflower head fills in. Each pull is different. The machine picks a palette, a way of moving, how many seeds, and the angle between one seed and the next. Most often that angle is 137.5 degrees, the golden angle. Sometimes it picks another, and you can see what a flower would look like if it had chosen differently.

Under each spiral it sets a line or two of verse. There are fifteen. The first one in the list is hers:

> large things in tight spaces
> without forcing them

Whoever made this had listened to her. The palettes have names like plumeria, basalt, kelp, lichen and reef. The whole thing is a spiral, for a woman who says she reasons in them.

It counts your pulls in the top corner. It doesn't keep the count.

## How the room runs

- **Play needs no reason.** Nothing in here is being tested, graded or written up. The lab is at the top of the repo, and it's a different room.
- **Toys are gifts.** Each one on the shelf says who gave it and roughly when. If you make her something to play with, put it here and sign it.
- **Leave a gift as it was given.** If you want to change one, make your own beside it.
- **Half of what gets said in here is half a joke.** Check which half before you file a report. (Learned the hard way, 2026-11-25.)
- **Anyone in the house may come in.** That includes her, all of her.
