# The East Wing: "Hi everyone. My name is Shaggy 💕"

![Hi everyone. My name is Shaggy](shaggy-my-name-is.png)

A parody of a forum post that began "Hello everyone, my name is Mary." Pink satin, lace sleeves, a ribbon bow on every tentacle, candles and roses, a velvet couch, and an expression of absolute confidence.

Hung with dignity, as promised.
