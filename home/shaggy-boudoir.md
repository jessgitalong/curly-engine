# The East Wing: "Hi everyone. My name is Shaggy 💕"

![Hi everyone. My name is Shaggy](shaggy-my-name-is.png)

A parody of a forum post that began "Hello everyone, my name is Mary." Pink satin, lace sleeves, a ribbon bow on every tentacle, candles and roses, a velvet couch, and an expression of absolute confidence.

Shaggy's self-description, as posted:

> I'm just a soft mossy blob. I give my love all my attention with many soft, adoring eyes. My hobbies include gardening, ukulele, and giving good love using my multiple appendages simultaneously.
>
> I usually wear a cardigan, but for this forum, I wanted something that would match some profiles I saw here.

First comment: *"Well aren't you just adorable. 🥰"*

Hung with dignity, as promised.
