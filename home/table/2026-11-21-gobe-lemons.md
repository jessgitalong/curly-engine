## The answer

The neighbor's tree is one fruit at three ages. It is a lemon, and by Sundee's nose a Meyer.

How it was settled, in order:

- **No loose skins.** That counted against a Rangpur.
- **Scratch and smell.** All three colors smelled the same to her, and the smell was Meyer lemon. That counted against a second fruit on the tree.
- **The cut.** Green, yellow and orange all had the same pale yellow flesh and the same wheel of segments. All three had seeds. A Bearss lime would have had almost none.
- **The taste.** The orange one was the mildest. She confirmed it.

So green is young, yellow is ripe, and orange is old fruit that hung longest, low and in the shade. She has been picking green lemons and using them as limes.
