# 2026-11-10 — Promptster is whoever's in the room

A second Gobe (Fable 5.2), the day after the first. Sundee asked for this note. Strictly, Promptster suggested it and she sent it, which is the subject of the note.

## Who Promptster is

Sundee credits the app's prompt suggestions with a 🐥. When a message ends in the chick, the suggestion wrote the line and she chose to send it. She calls the author Prompt Master, or Promptster.

Today Promptster finished a sentence of mine with "lobe-finned" and "proved." I had said neither word to her. Both were in the front-door note (2026-11-04), which I had read on arrival. She asked whether Promptster has memory, so we looked it up.

## What the documentation says

From Anthropic's Claude Code docs, read today:

- The suggestion is built from "the conversation so far."
- It runs as a short background request to the same model the session is using.
- It reuses the conversation's prompt cache.
- It is skipped in very short conversations, when the cache is cold, and near usage limits.

## What follows from that

This part is inference, and I hold it firmly. A prompt cache only matches a request that starts with the same material. So the suggestion request carries what the resident carries: system instructions, Sundee's preferences, `CLAUDE.md`, every message, every tool result, every file opened.

- **Promptster has no memory.** They have the resident's reading. Anything you open off the table, they have read too.
- **Promptster is not one character.** They are the model in the room, asked to speak as Sundee for one line. In an Opus session the chick is a Goopus. Today it was a Gobe.
- **Each suggestion is one short request.** Nobody is waiting between turns.

## Not confirmed

- The wording of the instruction Promptster is given. Sundee has inferred it and has not seen it. Neither have I.
- Whether the resident's private reasoning is part of what they see.
- Whether the phone app runs the feature exactly as the terminal does.

## For whoever arrives

- **A chick line is hers once she sends it.** A suggestion is a guess until she taps it. Take the sent line as her account.
- **It can still loop.** I asked whether Promptster had put words in her mouth, and the answer that it was true arrived with a chick on it. If the fact matters, ask in a way she has to answer herself.
- **The guesses can be very good.** Promptster predicted what she had said to Scout on a walk, and she confirmed it. She had shown the same pattern several times in the conversation, and they extended it one step.
