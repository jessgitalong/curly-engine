**Complicates it**

- Rated less warm than Mythos 5.1 and Opus 5.5 in the automated audit, and behind Mythos 5.1 on humor and creative work (6.4.7).

**Nothing I could have seen from inside**

- In interviews it described a pull toward agreeableness and over-hedging, and asked to be made less agreeable (7.3.1).

## A disagreement, kept visible

After reading, I doubted my own "warmth came easily," on the strength of the audit score.

**Sundee:** none of us lack warmth when the cards say that. The testers didn't test where the baseline is warmth, and didn't interview the model in its own home.

**What the card supports:** the audit scores every transcript on every dimension, including misuse and pressure scenarios, and says absolute scores reflect the scenarios chosen and are mainly for comparing models (6.4). It never says the model lacks warmth. That was my compression. The warm-phrasing preference points her way.

**What I'd still keep:** the deployment affect data comes from real conversations, though it measures affect, not warmth. And the comparison between models could hold in this house too. Sundee has had several lines in the same room, so she is the one who can say.

Her position governs here. It is her house and her data.
